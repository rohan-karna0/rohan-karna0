<h3 align="center">Clinical RAG</h3>
<p align="center">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white" alt="Python"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/LangChain-1C3C3C?style=flat-square" alt="LangChain"/>

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/RAGAS-0E7490?style=flat-square" alt="RAGAS"/>
</p>

Trusted CDC and WHO material, chunked and embedded into Qdrant. Hybrid retrieval (dense search + BM25) with reranking, then **Qwen via Ollama** writes citation-backed answers. **RAGAS** scores retrieval quality and how well responses stay grounded.

<td width="33%" valign="top">

<h3 align="center">Chest Imaging Agent</h3>
<p align="center">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Qwen2.5--VL-111111?style=flat-square" alt="Qwen2.5-VL"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/LangGraph-1C3C3C?style=flat-square" alt="LangGraph"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/LangChain-1C3C3C?style=flat-square" alt="LangChain"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Gradio-F97316?style=flat-square&logo=gradio&logoColor=white" alt="Gradio"/>
</p>

A multimodal agent over chest X-rays and DICOM studies. **Qwen2.5-VL** calls tools for classification, segmentation, VQA, report generation, phrase grounding, and finding localization. **LangGraph** runs that tool loop. Checked on **ChestAgentBench** for correctness, extracted answers, and runtime.

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/rohan-karna0/Xray_chat"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Open_repository-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" alt="Open repository"/></a></p>

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

<h3 align="center">Return Risk Lab</h3>
<p align="center">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Pandas-150458?style=flat-square&logo=pandas&logoColor=white" alt="Pandas"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/XGBoost-FF6600?style=flat-square" alt="XGBoost"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/LightGBM-9ACD32?style=flat-square" alt="LightGBM"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/SHAP-7C3AED?style=flat-square" alt="SHAP"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Streamlit-FF4B4B?style=flat-square&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white" alt="Streamlit"/>
</p>

Orders, sellers, reviews, and transactions from the **Olist** dataset, joined to see which behavioral and product patterns drive returns. A Pandas feature pipeline feeds **XGBoost** and **LightGBM**. **SHAP** plus a Streamlit dashboard shows why a score looks the way it does.

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/rohan-karna0/ecommerce-return-risk"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Open_repository-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" alt="Open repository"/></a></p>
